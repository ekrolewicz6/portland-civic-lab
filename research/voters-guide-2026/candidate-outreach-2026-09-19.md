# Council candidate outreach — September 19, 2026

Individual emails were sent from edan@portlandciviclab.org to all 33 candidates in the published Council guide: 21 in District 3 and 12 in District 4. All 33 messages were confirmed in Gmail Sent. This establishes sending, not delivery, reading or endorsement. No matching bounce notices were found immediately after sending; later delivery failures remain possible.

Recipients came from the City's public candidate contact list, retrieved September 19:
https://www.portland.gov/auditor/elections/documents/2026-candidate-public-contact-list/download

The contact sheet spells Matthias Hallett's name “Mattias.” His original filing confirms Matthias and the same primary email. Only each candidate's primary listed address was used. Secondary addresses and phone numbers were not contacted.

Each email explains the nonpartisan purpose, the same editorial standards for every candidate, the working edition's AI assistance and incomplete independent human review, and the invitation to correct errors, clarify positions, supply context or flag unfair language. Each includes a verified live profile link and candidate-specific questions derived from the current brief. Incumbents receive questions about directly sourced reasoning for recorded budget choices; candidates with missing issue summaries receive requests for those positions. All receive a request for concrete accomplishments, personal contribution, outcomes and supporting sources. Darren McCormick and John J Goldsmith also receive requests for a portrait with credit and permission.

The invitation promises to correct factual errors and amend or clarify misrepresentations. It does not promise automatic acceptance of unsupported factual claims or removal of documented conflicting evidence. Substantive policy answers are requested on the record. Participation is optional and lack of response is not evidence of lacking a position or experience.

Private working archive (git-ignored): `runtime-data/voters-guide-2026/outreach/`. `emails.md` preserves the messages; `messages.json` contains per-candidate questions, recipients, message IDs and body hashes; `sent-ledger.json` preserves send receipts. Do not resend without first consulting that ledger and Gmail Sent. The initial Sent search found no earlier matching guide outreach.

For subsequent guide updates, distinguish the September 18 research cutoff from the September 19 outreach date. Earlier statements that no candidates had been contacted describe the pre-outreach edition and must be updated when publishing the next edition. Keep campaign contact addresses and raw mailbox metadata out of the public guide. Assess responses individually; attribute new campaign statements, verify factual corrections, retain source dates and log material changes. No responses were incorporated during this outreach task.
