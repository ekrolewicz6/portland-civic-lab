```text
Subject: Portland Civic Lab voters guide: Chris Beck, U.S. House, Oregon District 2

Hi Chris,

I run Portland Civic Lab, a nonpartisan, volunteer research project publishing a voters guide for every race on the November 3 ballot. Your page is at https://www.portlandciviclab.org/voters-guide/oregon-house-2/chris-beck. It is built only from your published words (your filed statement, your site and public statements) and the public record, the same way for every candidate, with a source on every line. It carries no endorsements, rankings or scores. The race page also has a board for each live choice Congress faces, with every candidate’s statement or a gap: https://www.portlandciviclab.org/voters-guide/oregon-house-2#topics.

Right now the page shows 7 of 8 choices on record for you. A gap on a page is a research gap, not a position, and it says so, but readers do compare. The questions below are the gaps. A sentence or two on any of them is enough; we quote or paraphrase with a date and attribute it to your campaign, and we never fill a gap from a general goal.

The four issues every race page tracks
1. Public safety and immigration. What balance of enforcement, prevention and care do you propose?
2. Taxes, spending and your bills. Your page shows “Restore Medicaid funding.” We could not find how you would deliver it (the funding source, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partner), or what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.
3. Transportation, energy and climate. Your page shows “Restore wildfire funding.” We could not find what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.

Choices your published statements come close to
4. ACA premium credits. Restore the enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits that expired December 31, 2025? (Your page carries “Premiums skyrocketing” from your published statement; it does not say which way you come down on this choice.)
5. Fix Our Forests Act. Pass the Fix Our Forests Act: faster thinning and logging on federal forests with shorter windows to sue? (Your page carries “Restore Forest Service cuts” from your published statement; it does not say which way you come down on this choice.)
6. Federal housing programs. Expand the federal role in housing, from the new ROAD to Housing law to more vouchers and tax credits? (Your page carries “Rural housing programs” from your published statement; it does not say which way you come down on this choice.)

Choices we found no statement on
7. ICE and border money. Fund the $70 billion expansion of ICE and Border Patrol through 2029 (the Secure America Act)?

If any line on your page is wrong, out of date or unfair, say so and I will correct it and note the change in the research log. Anything you send on the record we treat the same as we treat every campaign’s statements. Thanks for your time.

Edan Krolewicz
Portland Civic Lab
edan@portlandciviclab.org
```
