## Tables NOT found (absent rows, not zeros)

- **bond_projects**: no per-project original-budget/current-estimate/spent table anywhere in the book. Fund 450 detail (pp.128-130) is account-level only. Narrative on p.34 says modernization program is "$89 to $125 million dollars below the amount needed" but gives no project table.
- **ADMr/ADMw**: not printed anywhere in the book; enrollment.csv carries headcount only.
- **AV / RMV / compression loss**: no assessed-value, real-market-value, or compression figures printed; tax_rates.csv left blank there.
- **SSF formula breakdown** (general purpose grant vs local revenue offset): not printed; only acct 431010 total. Narrative p.65 notes $4.7743 of the permanent rate is "local revenue" under the SSF formula and the gap tax raises ~$20M/yr, but no dollar table.
- **Bond levy rate**: bonded debt is levied as a dollar amount, $128,700,000 (Res. 5668 p.208, ED-50 line 4b p.212), not a rate.

## Anomalies

1. **Adopting resolution appropriates the Approved column, not the book's "Adopted" column.** Attachment A to both Resolution 5662 (Approved, p.206) and Resolution 5668 (Adopted, p.209) are identical and match the *Approved* figures (e.g. all-funds Instruction $398,932,000; Fund 101 Instruction $350,108,662). The summary tables' "Adopted" columns (pp.69-75) differ by a $250,000 shift from Instruction to Support Services (all-funds Instruction $398,682,000; GF Instruction $349,858,662, GF Support $272,497,648). Fund totals are unaffected.
2. **Printed GF tables misfoot by $1-2.** P.73/74 GF requirements components sum to $655,002,467 vs printed total $655,002,466 (+$1). Fund-detail account subtotals (pp.89-92) sum to $655,002,465 (-$1). P.75 "Materials and Services" adopted $100,689,608 vs $100,689,606 summed from detail subtotals (+$2). P.75 "Debt Service & Other" 2016/17 actual $3,708,513 vs detail "Subtotal - Other Accounts" $3,707,442 — the $1,071 difference equals the 562000 Interest line, which the detail subtotal apparently omits.
3. **Typo in GF narrative total**: p.73 says "The total General Fund Budget for FY 2018/19 is $655,022.466" — should be $655,002,466. Resolution 5662 p.205 says "fiscal year 20118/19" and its Recital B says the budget message was for "fiscal year 2017/18" (both typos for 2018/19).
4. **Funds 322 (FF&C QZAB) and 323 (FF&C Taxable) folded into Fund 320** for FY2018/19: they appear as separate destination funds in the FY2017/18 transfers table (p.63) and still appear as separate payment rows in the FY2018/19 Payments by Debt Service Fund table (p.78: Fund 322 $200,000, Fund 323 $418,826), but the district fund list (p.62) and fund details show only Fund 320, whose adopted total $1,846,785 = $1,227,959 + $200,000 + $418,826.
5. **Fund 470 Partnership Funds zeroed out**: fund concluded June 30, 2018 (p.131); budgeted at $0 for 2018/19 but still listed.
6. **GF summary vs detail classification**: the p.72 summary folds acct 412000 Rev-Local Gov't Not Districts ($8,400) out of Revenue from Taxes and Food Service Sales ($105,000) into "Other Local Sources," so summary Revenue from Taxes ($355,943,637) is $8,400 below the detail subtotal ($355,952,037), and summary Other Local ($8,932,822) = detail Other Local $8,819,422 + food service $105,000 + $8,400. Actual-year rows in gf_revenue.csv follow the detail classification (only place actuals are printed).
7. **All-funds resources table has no actual-year columns** (p.68 is 4-column only), so all-funds resources-by-source actuals for 2015/16-2016/17 are not extractable from this book; per-fund actual totals were taken from each fund detail instead.
8. **Cover/basis label mix**: PDF p.1 header says "Approved Budget" while the rest of the book is headed "Adopted Budget". Figures were taken per-column, so no impact.
9. **FTE tables are 2018/19-only and flagged preliminary** ("should not be considered final ... until after the beginning of the school year", p.45); no prior-year FTE comparison table at function level. School-level FTE-change tables (pp.39-42) exist but are change-oriented and were not fragmented.
10. Fund 350 is printed as "GO Bonds Debt Service Funds" (plural) on p.62 and "Fund 350 - GO Bonds Debt Service Fund" in the TOC/detail; normalized to the singular in the fragment.

## Basis mapping used

Actual 2015/16 and 2016/17 = actual-budgetary; Current 2017/18 = revised; Proposed/Approved/Adopted 2018/19 as labeled. Book prints whole dollars everywhere; fragments are in thousands.
