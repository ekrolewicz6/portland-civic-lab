## Note for the vote layer: data centers

On September 17, 2026, all 12 councilors co-sponsored and passed a resolution urging the City Administrator not to sign data-center NDAs and stating that Council "intends to pursue a moratorium or zoning restrictions on all hyperscale data centers" (City release: https://www.portland.gov/council/districts/3/tiffany-koyama-lane/news/2026/9/17/historic-first-all-12-portland-city; WW September 2 quotes the Koyama Lane–Green amendment language). The `data-centers` topic has no `decisionId`, so Clark, Green and Zimmerman show no pill there. That is a recorded action, not a statement, so nothing was added here; a decision entry in `council-decisions` would let the vote render for all three incumbents.

## Least sure (review these first)

1. **Olivia Clark × police staffing ("supports").** Her April 16 post says the bureau is "one of the nation's most understaffed" and "unacceptable"; it does not say "hire more officers." A reviewer may prefer "partial" with the hiring question named. Her June 24 post ("a fully staffed police force") and site ("protect core police funding") point the same way.
2. **Jamey Evenstar × Moda ("partial").** She speaks to terms (terrible original deal, better amendments, Albina benefits, escalating rent) but never to whether public money should go in. "Mixed" is the alternative reading; the sentence is built from three posts, with the August 12 post cited.
3. **Matt Schulte × camps ("partial").** The only sentence is "When the City, like in a camp sweep, causes the disruption, it has an obligation to fix it." It is about sweeps, but thin on the funding choice.
4. **Josh Leake × Street Response ("supports", Reporting).** The Mercury paraphrases his questionnaire ("expanding Portland Street Response to 24/7 service"); his own words are not printed and his site does not repeat it.
5. **Matt Schulte × Moda ("partial").** A verbatim questionnaire quote about negotiating posture; it answers the "terms" half of the column, not the money half.
6. **Jamey Evenstar × Street Response ("partial").** Reverses the September 20 decision to leave the cell empty. The FAQ still does not name Street Response; the July 22 post does, but only about a lease.
7. **Jeremy Beausoleil Smith × taxes ("supports").** The source is a quiz-style post ("Should we Tax the Rich in Portland? Yes"); the platform adds corporations' "fair share" with no instrument. Treated like Green's pamphlet line, which is recorded as "supports."
8. **Eli Arnold × Moda ("supports", Reporting).** The NW Examiner quote pairs "securing the best possible deal with public funds is important" with "moving forward on this project is also crucial." Read as support for proceeding with public funds; a reviewer may prefer "mixed."
