# First governor debate, October 1, 2026 (as reported by Willamette Week)

- Article: Penny Rosenberg, "First Oregon Governor's Debate Gets Feisty," Willamette Week, October 2, 2026, 7:13 pm PT. https://www.wweek.com/news/state/2026/10/02/first-oregon-governors-debate-gets-feisty/
- Debate: Thursday, October 1, 2026, University of Oregon, Eugene. Moderators: Nigel Jaquiss (Oregon Journalism Project), Ben Botkin (Lookout Eugene-Springfield), Bayla Orton (UO student journalist). On stage: Tina Kotek and Christine Drazan. Brett Smith did not take part.

## Passages used (verbatim from the article)
- Vote by mail: "Kotek said she wouldn't change the current system, while Drazan said she wants to return polling booths to Oregon." And: "While in the House in 2025, Drazan sponsored House Bill 3872, which would have made in-person voting the 'normal method for voting' and offered absentee ballots only to voters who couldn't make it to the polls on election day. It also required showing government ID ... It did not advance."
- Climate Protection Program: Kotek, "I am 100% supportive of our current Climate Protection Program because it is going to help us reduce our carbon pollution here in the state," and "I am supportive of the Legislature taking this up next year to figure out how we might be able to be more competitive." The paper says she "hedged" when asked about her Prosperity Council's recommendation (replace the program with cap-and-invest) and that the program stays in place meanwhile. Drazan: "I will rescind Tina Kotek's Climate Protection Program on day one.…We cannot afford to continue to advance a political agenda that harms families and makes us uniquely expensive."
- Data centers: "Both candidates now support a moratorium, and neither candidate was able to articulate a clear policy." Kotek "will introduce a bill requiring the centers to produce their own energy, and that it be clean energy, built by union labor, and that they provide benefits in those communities" on day one if reelected.

## Verified against the record
- HB 3872 (2025 regular session), OLIS https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2025R1/Measures/Overview/HB3872: chief sponsor Representative Drazan (print order 1). Digest: "Makes voting in person the normal method for voting. Requires voters to show picture ID when voting or requesting a ballot. Refers the Act to the people at the next general election." Last location: House Committee on Rules (no further action).
- Measure 60 (1998) established vote by mail for all Oregon elections (board context).
