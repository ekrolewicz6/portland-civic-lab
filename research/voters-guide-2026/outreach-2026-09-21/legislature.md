## Paste block: what the ladder is

> Our race page shows each documented position as a three-rung ladder: **what** you would do, **how** you would deliver it (the money, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partnership), and **measured by** (the number, result or date that would show it worked). We fill the second and third rungs only from your own words. Where your materials do not yet say, the page shows a gap and, once we have asked you, says “asked.” The questions below name exactly those gaps. A sentence or two per question is enough; we quote or paraphrase with attribution and link the reply. Where an issue has no position at all, we say so rather than guess.
