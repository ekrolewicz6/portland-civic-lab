# Outreach draft · Washington County packs (county-washington, city-washington)

Drafted September 21, 2026 from the November 2026 Washington County voters’ pamphlet and each campaign’s own site or filing. Nothing here has been sent. Each entry lists the channel found, the ladder gaps (issue by issue: which of *how* / *measure* is missing, as a question) and any of the four issues (housing, safety, money, streets/climate) with no published position at all.

Shared question for every campaign: *For each promise below, what is the mechanism (money, rule, staffing, partner) and what result, number or date would show it worked?*
