# First landscape screening · September 30, 2026

No landscape has qualified as the pilot yet. A candidate needs an identifiable project boundary, a completed activity with attributable expenditure records, and a custodian able to clarify the records. Score qualified candidates on finance documentation (30), activity/geography links (25), custodian access (20), monitoring (15), and community relevance (10). Do not assign unearned points for records merely requested.

| Candidate | Public starting evidence | Missing gate evidence | First focused question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ashland/Rogue Valley | [City AFR monitoring](https://ashlandoregon.gov/201/Science-Monitoring), [accomplishments](https://ashlandoregon.gov/166/Accomplishments-Monitoring-Summary), [2025 CWPP](https://www.ashlandoregon.gov/DocumentCenter/View/4053/250624_Ashland_CWPP_FINAL) | A project-level expenditure chain and current custodian of those records | Which completed AFR treatment has a unit boundary, contract or expenditure ledger, and follow-up monitoring? |
| Central Oregon/Deschutes | [2023 collaborative report](https://www.fs.usda.gov/restoration/documents/cflrp/2023AnnualReports/FY2023%20Deschutes%20CFLRP%20Annual%20Report_Final%20Feb%2028%202024.pdf), [ODF 2025 implementation report](https://www.oregon.gov/odf/aboutodf/documents/2025-odf-sb83-landscape-resilency-strategy-implementation-report.pdf) | A bounded project linking a funding line to completed units and work hours | Which completed unit can be traced through decision, preparation, contract, and accomplishment? |
| Wallowa/northeastern Oregon | [Wallowa Resources program overview](https://www.wallowaresources.org/), [USFS CFLRP national report](https://www.fs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/fs_media/fs_document/CFLRP-15-year-report.pdf) | Project-specific finance, geometry and outcome records | Which recent project has documented expenditure and repeat-treatment history? |

Public accomplishments are useful leads, not a joined project account. Ashland's published job and acreage totals should not be divided into a cost per treated acre without a common scope. The ODF statewide report includes programs with substantially different purposes and accounting bases; its $50,984,180 total is not a denominator for prescribed-fire cost.

At the two-week selection gate, record the source and score for every criterion, date of every custodian response, the qualifying project boundary and activity, and the reason for selection. If nobody qualifies, publish the guide revision and continue acquisition.
