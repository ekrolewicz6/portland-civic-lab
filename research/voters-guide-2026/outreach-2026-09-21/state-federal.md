Source data: `src/lib/voters-guide/race-sheet/content/packs/state.ts` and `federal.ts`. Nothing here has been sent.

## Paste block: what the ladder is

> Our race page shows each documented position as a three-rung ladder: **what** you would do, **how** you would deliver it (the money, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partnership), and **measured by** (the number, result or date that would show it worked). We fill the second and third rungs only from your own words. Where your materials do not yet say, the page shows a gap and, once we have asked you, says “asked.” The questions below name exactly those gaps, plus any of our four issues (rent and homes; camps, crime and who responds; your bills and taxes; streets, buses and air) on which we found no position of yours at all. A sentence or two per question is enough; we quote or paraphrase with attribution and link the reply.
