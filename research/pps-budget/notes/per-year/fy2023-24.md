# FY2023-24 extraction worksheet

Corpus: `budget-fy2023-24-vol1.txt` (305 PDF pages), `budget-fy2023-24-vol2.txt` (180 PDF pages).
Vol2 is Individual School Reports only — no district-level financial tables; every extracted figure
comes from vol1. Printed page = PDF page − 7 throughout vol1.

**Adopted totals as printed:** All Funds $2,182,057K (PDF p86-88); General Fund $833,774K (PDF p94-96).

## Year-columns in the multi-year tables

Every summary and fund-detail table carries: Actual 2019-20, Actual 2020-21, Actual 2021-22,
Budget 2022-23 (+ Budget FTE 2022-23), Proposed 2023-24, Approved 2023-24, Adopted 2023-24
(+ Budget FTE 2023-24). The 2022-23 "Budget" column equals the FY2022-23 book's adopted total
(1,883,261, verified against budget-fy2022-23-vol1 PDF p140), so it is emitted as basis=adopted,
not revised. A row was emitted per year-column in every fragment.

## Anomalies

1. **Text layer doubles/overlaps** on the summary pages (86-88, 94-96): the PDF renders the same
   table twice, interleaved. Each of these pages also carries one clean single-spaced copy of every
   row; values were read from the clean copies and foot-checked (see 9).
2. **FY2021-22 GF actuals are inflated ~$400M** by the July 2021 Series 2021 Full Faith and Credit
   Pension Bonds: resources include 451100 Bond Proceeds 399,390 + 451200 Premium 1,449 (p98);
   requirements include ~400,833 booked to function 5100/object 600 (p95/96). GF 2021-22 total
   1,188,261 vs ~744-834K in adjacent years. The 10-year GF actuals series must treat 2021-22
   accordingly.
3. **GF proposed 2023-24 total printed three ways**: 823,424 (p94 resources), 823,422 (p95
   function table), 823,423 (p98/99 detail) — book's own rounding disclaimer applies.
4. **New fund 437 Harriet Tubman MS Relocation Fund**, first budgeted 2023-24 at $122,500K, shown
   as Restricted Grants/Funds in the p194 category table. Funds 407 (IT System Project) and 470
   (Partnership) are dormant ($0 since 2019-20 actuals).
5. **Fund 191 appears only in the p194 Other Capital Projects matrix** ($996,606 adopted 2023-24)
   with no fund summary section of its own in the Financial Section.
6. **Two AV figures for 2023-24**: Revenue Factors p27 "Total Net Assessed Value" $65.61B vs
   Assessed Value page p208 fiscal 2024 estimate $63.86B — different measures/timing; p27 used
   in tax_rates.csv, noted.
7. **Permanent rate presentation**: p40 prints "$5.2781 per $1,000" as the permanent rate, which is
   the operations total = permanent 4.7743 + Measure 5 gap 0.5038 (split per p210).
8. **Reserves trajectory as narrated (p87)**: contingency 56.0M → 48.4M; unappropriated EFB
   46.5M → 26.3M from 2022-23 adopted to 2023-24 adopted.
9. **Cross-table rounding (±1K)**: individual funds sum to 2,182,058 vs printed all-funds adopted
   total 2,182,057; capital group 2019-20 876,613 vs 876,615 (duplicate print); cafeteria 2021-22
   resources 26,237 vs requirements 26,236; internal service 2022-23 8,646 vs 8,645; GF function
   sum adopted 833,773 vs 833,774. All within the book's rounding disclaimer.
10. **FTE 6,246.46 labeled two ways**: "Budget FTE 2023-24" beside the Adopted column (p87) and
    "2024 Proposed" in the personnel section (p220) — same figure; recorded once as adopted
    (p87) and once as proposed (p220) in fte_by_function.csv with a note.
11. **ADMw variants for 2023-24**: projected ADMw 52,720 (budget assumption, p26/27); ADMw
    Extended 53,352 (p27); ODE Extended ADMw 53,351.68 and CTA ADMw Ext 53,698.94 on the MESD
    service plan (p204).
12. No Measure 98 sub-fund split in this year (that begins FY2025-26+); High School Success (M98)
    is inside the Grants Fund (205).
