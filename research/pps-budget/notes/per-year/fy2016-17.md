## Not found / not printed

- **ADMr/ADMw:** no numeric figures anywhere in the book — only definitions (glossary) and narrative ("reasonable current estimates of ADMw", forecast +650 students, p64). enrollment.csv carries only the October 2015 headcount (49,075).
- **AV/RMV/compression dollars:** not printed. Compression is discussed only as percentages (loss reduced to 21% in 2015/16; a further 3.5pp reduction assumed for 2016/17, p65). tax_rates.csv av/rmv/compression left blank.
- **SSF GP grant vs local revenue offset split:** not printed; only the single 431010 line ($218,306,690 adopted). Narrative: 2015-17 biennium appropriation $7.376B final (p64; p63 says $7.255B + $105M added later).
- **levy.csv skipped:** no single printed imposed/collected local-option dollar figure. Rate $1.9900 (in tax_rates.csv); narrative forecast "approximately $82 million in 2016/17" (p68); account-level CY local option lines are in the Fund 101 resources detail (p87: 80,057,000 Mult + 64,000 Clack + 459,000 Wash adopted). No positions-funded figure.
- **Bonded debt levy is a dollar amount, not a rate:** $50,778,368 (Resolutions p208/p211, ED-50 p215).
- **GF tax reconciliation table:** the Fund 101 intro (p87) promises "a table reconciling taxes to be received and imposed" after the resources table, but no such table appears in the text layer — possibly dropped from the PDF or lost in text extraction.

## Anomalies

- **Dollar-rounding discrepancies in printed totals:** GF 2013/14 actual total prints as 516,645,666 in the program/account category summaries (pp.79-80) but 516,645,665 in the Fund 101 detail (pp.89, 94, 98); 2013/14 EFB prints 51,673,785 (p79) vs 51,673,784 (p94). Kept each as printed, noted in-row.
- **Fund 338 (Facilities Capital Debt Service) is new in 2016/17** and first appears at the *approved* stage ($323,530; dash in Current and Proposed columns) — services the 2017 QZAB.
- **Fund 420 (Full Faith and Credit Fund) winding down:** $55,380 in Current 2015/16, dash in all 2016/17 columns.
- **Funds 306 (Settlement DS) and 309 (SELP DS) have TOC/detail pages but no rows in the by-fund table (p66)** — zero budgets.
- **Approved→Adopted changes:** GF grew $557,071 (592,043,109→592,600,180); Grants Fund +$326,914; GO Bonds DS shrank at approval (50,128,598→49,718,598). Adopted GF contingency $15,233,717 is $400,000 below approved.
- **Object categories print combined:** the GF account-category table combines Salaries+Benefits (100+200) and Materials and Services (300+400). True 100/200 split taken from the printed Fund 101 subtotals (p95); no printed single 300 or 400 totals (only eight purchased-services subtotals + one supplies subtotal in the detail, pp.95-97) — not summed, per house rule.
- **FTE table caveat:** p57 warns staffing allocations are "very preliminary"; the table is adopted-year only (no prior-year FTE columns anywhere in the book).
- **bond_projects amounts are single-year:** Fund 450 by Major Project (p146) prints FY2016/17 appropriations only — no original budgets, life-of-project estimates, or spent-to-date. Placed in current_estimate_k with explicit notes. Bond = 2012 $482M authorization, 2013 & 2015 issuances.
- **Debt schedule:** no combined GO-outstanding line; principal totals print per issue (2013 GO $67.02M, 2015 GO $244.7M, Limited Tax Pension & Refunding $384.07M combined, IT $7.6M, 2010 RZ $6.7M, QZAB $5.5M; grand total $715,594,171). FY2016/17 debt service $97,171,667 across funds 307/308/320/338/350.
- **Basis mapping:** "Current 2015/16" columns treated as `revised` (the 2015/16 budget was amended Jan/Feb 2016, p31); prior "Actual" columns as `actual-budgetary`.
- **Contingency composition:** adopted GF contingency includes assigned and self-insurance pieces; unassigned portion is 2.475% of expenditures, below the 3% board policy (pp.63-65).
