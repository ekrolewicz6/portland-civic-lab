# What was sent (September 25, 2026)

89 emails went out from edan@portlandciviclab.org on the afternoon of September 25, 2026, one at a time, 25 to 60 seconds apart at random, each with its own subject line: 25 to news outlets and reporters, 41 to issue and advocacy groups, 23 to civic groups, neighborhood coalitions, universities and election offices. Each asks one question tied to the recipient's own work and links the voters guide as `PortlandCivicLab.org/voters-guide/...`. None bounced. The per-message ledger (recipient, subject, Gmail message and thread ids) is private and stays in `runtime-data/voters-guide-2026/outreach/2026-09-25-orgs-results.jsonl`; the full text of every email is in `2026-09-25-orgs-plan.json` beside it.

The research behind the recipient list is in `news.md`, `issue-groups.md`, `advocacy-business.md` and `civic.md` in this folder.
