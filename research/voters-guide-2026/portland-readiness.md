# Portland Council publication assessment

Reviewed September 18, 2026. This is an editorial assessment of the working edition, not a claim that human review or campaign outreach has occurred.

## Recommendation

The two Council pages can support a clearly labeled initial research edition. They cannot yet support a claim of exhaustive candidate investigation. The user authorized publication of the Council working edition. The release is restricted to Districts 3 and 4; broader research is excluded from public routes and exports.

- Complete official field: District 3, 21 candidates; District 4, 12 candidates. Source: [City certified register](https://www.portland.gov/auditor/elections/run4office/2026-city-candidates).
- Thirty substantive current policy briefs, plus three explicitly limited profiles.
- Thirty-one Council portraits; one additional portrait for auditor Simone Rede. Twenty-seven Council portraits were extracted from candidate-submitted 2026 Multnomah pamphlet entries. Additional portraits come from named civic-event/news directories. Campaign sites were sought first for the remaining candidates; several were missing, inaccessible, or lacked a suitable portrait.
- Every candidate receives the same alphabetical presentation and profile structure. No default comparison selection or recommendation. Original image colors preserved; display frames may crop edges. No synthetic portraits or retouching.
- All six incumbent councilors have the same final Resolution 37750 roll-call check. One vote is explicitly not characterized as their whole record.
- Source types, editorial interpretation, unanswered questions, review date and correction path are visible. Export includes portrait provenance alongside policy evidence.
