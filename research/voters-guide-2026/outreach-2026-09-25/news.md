# News outlets and reporters covering the November 3, 2026 races (researched September 25, 2026)

Every address below was printed on the outlet's own site, contact page, author page or article byline data. Nothing was guessed. Blocked or unconfirmed contacts are marked.

## Outlets that ran 2026 questionnaires or forums (the guide cites some)
- Portland Mercury: "Meet the Candidates" D3 (Sept 9) and D4 (Sept 11), built on its questionnaire; cited by the guide.
- OPB: May 2026 questionnaire PDFs for Washington County chair and Clackamas commission; cited. Joint OPB/Oregonian 2024 council questionnaire also cited. No November 2026 OPB questionnaire found.
- Oregon Capital Chronicle: primary voter guide (statewide, congressional, legislative); no general-election edition yet.
- KATU "Know Your Candidates": same questions for every candidate; 2026 entries cover only the governor primary.
- Street Roots: May 2026 primary questionnaire; nothing for November yet.
- KBOO "Running On Air 2026": hour-long D3 and D4 forums (9/22, 9/24, 9/29, 10/1).
- Pamplin/Carpenter community papers: candidate profile series for suburban city races.
