# FY2021-22 extraction worksheet

Source: `budget-fy2021-22-vol1.txt` (381 PDF pages) and `budget-fy2021-22-vol2.txt` (176 PDF pages).
Page offset in vol1: PDF page = printed page + 7 (e.g. printed 135 = PDF 142).
Vol2 is "School Reports Volume #2" — individual school staffing/budget pages only; no district-level
summary tables, so every fragment cites vol1.

## Adopted totals as printed

- All funds adopted 2021-22: **$2,058,410 thousand** (Resolution No. 6323, PDF p.373: "in the total
  amount of $2,058,410,000"; matches TOTAL RESOURCES p.142 and TOTAL REQUIREMENTS p.143).
- General Fund adopted 2021-22: **$771,963 thousand** (p.151 resources, p.152/153 requirements,
  Attachment A p.375).

## Not found

- **Headcount enrollment history**: "Student Enrollment History and Projected Enrollment" (PDF 302)
  is narrative + chart images with no extractable numbers; only ADMr/ADMw are in text (p.55, corroborated
  by the MESD service-plan sheet p.274: ODE Extended ADMw 57,311.39, CTA ADMr 48,056.00 as of 4/15/2021).
- **SSF general purpose grant / local revenue offset split**: only the net 431010 SSF--General Support
  line is printed (adopted 256,417k). Budget assumption p.54 gives the estimated first-year SSF
  distribution as $246.35M on a $9.3B state K-12 budget.
- **Local option positions funded**: not printed anywhere in vol1 (levy narrative p.51 says it "funds
  teaching positions and classroom supports" without a count).
- **Local option collections (actual)**: only levied amounts (p.278) and budgeted collections
  (104,279k adopted, p.151).
- **ED-50 assessed-value/compression detail**: form (p.378) carries rates and the $146,000,000 bond
  levy but no AV or compression figures.

## Anomalies

1. **Internal Service (600) requirements-by-function table not updated for adoption**: p.267 prints
   Approved/Adopted total 8,552k, but resources (p.266), requirements-by-object (p.268) and
   Attachment A (p.375) all print 8,565k — the +13k contingency adjustment (4,774 → 4,787) never made
   it into the by-function summary. Fragments carry 8,565 from p.268 with a note.
2. **Rounding mismatches (do not foot exactly)**: GF 2019-20 resources 709,093 vs requirements
   709,092; all-funds 2019-20 resources 1,923,579 vs requirements 1,923,580; all-funds 2019-20
   unappropriated 782,953 (p.143) vs 782,951 (p.144); Debt Service 2019-20 resources 191,558 vs
   requirements 191,557 and 2020-21 201,671 vs 201,672; Internal Service 2020-21 budget total prints
   8,533 (p.266) / 8,531 (p.267) / 8,532 (p.268). Book's own footnote: "Certain numbers have been
   rounded up or down."
3. **Attachment A vs summary-table cent-level rounding**: GF 3000 proposed prints 2,394 in
   Attachment A (p.375) vs 2,393 in the p.152 summary; adopted 2,994 vs 2,993.
4. **Debt service appropriation vs payment schedule**: Fund 300 appropriation is 200,309k
   (Attachment A p.375) while the payments-by-fund schedule (p.287) totals 200,307k.
5. **Gap tax folded into permanent rate**: the district levies a single statutory rate of $5.2781
   (resolution p.373, ED-50 p.378) = original permanent $4.7743 + gap $0.5038 (p.51, p.280);
   $0.5038 is excluded from urban-renewal division-of-tax calculations. tax_rates.csv keeps the
   components from p.280.
6. **School renames**: Madison HS → McDaniel HS, Wilson HS → Ida B Wells HS (pp.259-260);
   bond_projects rows keep the printed names with rename notes.
7. **2017 bond EH&S lines have no original budget** — allocations were made from the program budget;
   original_budget_k left blank for those rows.
8. **PDF text layer duplication**: many landscape tables print each line twice, interleaved
   (two overlapping text runs). Values were cross-checked between the duplicate runs.
9. No Measure 98 sub-fund split in this year (that begins FY2025-26+); High School Success (M98) is
   inside the Special Revenue grants fund. Fund structure this year: 100, 200 (201/202/205/225/251/299),
   300 (307/308/320/350), 400 (404/407/420/435/438/445/450/470), 600 (601).
