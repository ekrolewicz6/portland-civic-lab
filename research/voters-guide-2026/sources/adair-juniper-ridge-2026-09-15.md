# Patti Adair's county record on homelessness (sources suggested by Braver Angels, September 28, 2026)

## Used: Bend Bulletin, September 15, 2026
https://bendbulletin.com/2026/09/15/deschutes-county-to-fund-juniper-ridge-safe-camp-through-june/
- Deschutes County commissioners approved $275,000 to keep the temporary Juniper Ridge safe stay area operating through June 2027.
- "Commissioners Phil Chang and Patti Adair voted for the funding Monday afternoon, while Commissioner Tony DeBone was against it."
- Adair: "I was really hoping that we would have been more successful with more people at this point."
- Used for Adair's housing column on the OR-5 page (position, line, chip).

## Not used: Bend Source, June 8, 2026
https://www.bendsource.com/news/localnews/homeless-service-hub-proposed-for-east-redmond/
- Jericho Road proposes a 2,500-square-foot homeless service hub on 1.7 acres of county land in east Redmond ($1 million to build, $550,000 a year to run).
- County commissioners approved a letter of support on June 3, contingent on the nonprofit securing most of the funding first. The article does not give Adair's vote on the letter.
- Adair's only mention: at the April 6 meeting she "asked how many people had actually moved out of homelessness through the Oasis Village shelter program."
- Left out because it records a question, not a position or a vote.
