Recommendations:
• Advance the PSU performing arts center
• Form a Technical Advisory Committee to support pre-development financial functions
• While PSU development is ongoing, maintain Broadway shows at the Keller 
• At the same time, initiate planning for the Keller’s future—including financial analysis, market 
feasibility, and design—to allow for construction to begin once the PSU venue opens 
• When the PSU venue becomes operational, transition Broadway productions there, and begin 
renovation of the Keller into a modern 1,200–1,800 seat venue tailored to Portland’s mid-size 
market needs
Ex Officio Table Recommendations

Officio table recommended a path forward for this work in June 2026.

July 28, 2026
Proposed Resolution:  Next Steps for PSU venue
Directs Deputy City Administrator to:
• Stand up a staff project team of technical experts with PSU to complete pre-development work
• Develop a detailed financial plan with a fully defined capital stack, funding timelines, and return 
on invested capital that provides a clear understanding of the City’s obligations and costs for the 
project 
• Direct a cohesive, comprehensive philanthropic strategy, collaborate with the PSU Foundation in 
its role as lead philanthropic strategist, and work together to identify and secure necessary 
funding
• Provide input into the initial phases of the project and project construction management direction
• Develop a Project Commitment Agreement with PSU by Dec 1, 2026, that establishes the scope, 
schedule, and governance framework for the project and a shared commitment between the City and 
PSU to develop and implement the full capital funding strategy
• Designate a pre-development steering committee to participate in the process as needed 5
Affirms the City’s commitment to a new City-owned ~3,000-seat Broadway capable venue as part 
of the proposed Performing Arts + Culture Center at PSU with goal of opening in 2030
