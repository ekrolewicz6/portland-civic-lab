```text
Subject: Portland Civic Lab voters guide: Mark Shull, Clackamas County Commissioner, Position 2

Hi Mark,

I run Portland Civic Lab, a nonpartisan, volunteer research project publishing a voters guide for every race on the November 3 ballot. Your page is at https://www.portlandciviclab.org/voters-guide/clackamas-position-2/mark-shull. It is built only from your published words (your filed statement, your site and public statements) and the public record, the same way for every candidate, with a source on every line. It carries no endorsements, rankings or scores. The race page also has a board for each live choice Clackamas County faces, with every candidate’s statement or a gap: https://www.portlandciviclab.org/voters-guide/clackamas-position-2#topics.

Right now the page shows 4 of 5 choices on record for you. A gap on a page is a research gap, not a position, and it says so, but readers do compare. The questions below are the gaps. A sentence or two on any of them is enough; we quote or paraphrase with a date and attribute it to your campaign, and we never fill a gap from a general goal.

The four issues every race page tracks
1. Homelessness and housing. Your page shows “State land-use reform.” We could not find how you would deliver it (the funding source, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partner), or what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.
2. Crisis care, jail and deflection. How would you handle addiction, crisis calls and jail capacity?
3. Taxes and the budget gap. Your page shows “Senior tax relief, cuts.” We could not find how you would deliver it (the funding source, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partner), or what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.
4. Roads, bridges and air. Your page shows “No I-205 tolls.” We could not find how you would deliver it (the funding source, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partner), or what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.

Choices your published statements come close to
5. Recovery Campus. Back the Recovery Campus with county-issued bonds and operating money so it opens in fall 2027? (Your page carries “Build on Stabilization Center” from your published statement; it does not say which way you come down on this choice.)

Choices we found no statement on
6. Data-center moratorium. Adopt a county moratorium on data centers now, or wait for the 2027 Legislature as staff recommended?

If any line on your page is wrong, out of date or unfair, say so and I will correct it and note the change in the research log. Anything you send on the record we treat the same as we treat every campaign’s statements. Thanks for your time.

Edan Krolewicz
Portland Civic Lab
edan@portlandciviclab.org
```
