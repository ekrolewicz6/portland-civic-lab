## Tables NOT found

  (pp.63-64, 66) but no weighted/resident ADM figures. enrollment.csv admr/admw empty.
- **Assessed value / RMV / compression loss in dollars**: not printed. Tax base
  discussed only as rates. tax_rates.csv av/rmv/compression columns empty.
- **SSF formula detail** (general purpose grant vs local revenue offset): not printed;
  only GF account 431010 (state cash) and 431030 Common School Fund ($6,190,000 adopted).

  FY2017/18 annual budgets only — carried in current_estimate_k with a CAUTION note.

## Anomalies

1. **$200k typo on PDF p.71** ("Requirements (All Funds)"): Current 2016/17 column
   prints Facility Acq & Construction $272,943,725 and total $1,161,674,864; the
   by-fund table (p.65) and 6-column table (p.73) both print $272,743,725 /
   $1,161,474,864. The p.65/p.73 figure is used.
2. Proposed GF total was $616,286,841; Approved/Adopted are $617,286,841 — the $1.0M
   step is the MESD/ESD apportionment revision ($7.5M proposed -> $8.5M, account
   421020, p.89).
3. Budget Overview GF resources summary (p.75) folds Food Service Sales ($103,923)
   into "Other Local Sources" ($7,941,960 = $7,838,037 + $103,923 of the fund-detail
   subtotals). Fragments follow the fund-detail split.
4. Funds 322 (FF&C QZAB) and 323 (FF&C Taxable) receive GF transfers ($200,000 and
   $431,973, p.80) and appear in the debt-payments table (p.83), but are absent from
   the fund list (p.65) and appropriation schedules — their debt service is folded
   into Fund 320's appropriation ($1,859,707 = 1,247,734 + 200,000 + 411,973).
5. Fund 350 is titled "GO Bonds Debt Service Funds" (plural) in the by-fund table.
6. Adopted all-funds contingency of $441.3M is dominated by $412.4M "budgetary
   contingency" in Fund 450 (unspent May 2017 $790M bond capacity) — not an
   operating reserve. GF operating contingency is $23,514,287 (4% of expenditures);
   GF budgets no ending fund balance in 2016/17-2017/18.
7. debt.csv go_outstanding_k (=$1,129,029k) is the sum of three printed per-issue
   principal totals (2013 GO $65,315k + 2015 GO $211,555k + 2017 GO $852,159k, p.83);
   the 2017 GO column includes the planned first issuance of the May 2017 $790M
   authorization. All-obligation principal total prints $1,521,236,067.
8. Levy positions_funded (827) is CBRC arithmetic ($81,016,899 receipts as of
   2017-04-12 at $98,017 avg teacher cost), not a district staffing count.
9. Bonded debt levy imposed as a dollar amount ($124,300,000, Res. 5467 / ED-50
   p.212), not a rate — tax_rates.csv bond_rate left empty.
10. FTE table (p.55) carries the book's own caveat that staffing allocations are
    "very preliminary"; only the adopted year is presented (no prior-year FTE series
    in this book).
11. No fund renames/renumbering observed vs adjacent years; Fund 470 Partnership
    Funds (created 2015) and Fund 225 PERS Rate Stabilization present. Pre-Measure-98
    era: no sub-fund split.
