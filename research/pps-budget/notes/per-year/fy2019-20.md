## Anomalies

- **Resources vs requirements rounding**: adopted all-funds resources 1,379,458 vs requirements 1,379,456; GF 691,769 vs 691,767; SRF 137,409 vs 137,408; Grants Fund 67,526 vs 67,525. The book carries an explicit rounding disclaimer under every summary table. Appropriation resolution (p415) prints GF 1000 = 371,970 and 2000 = 289,512 vs 371,969 / 289,511 in the p83 table.
- **2018-19 column basis ambiguous**: headed simply "Budget 2018-19"; recorded as `revised` per project convention, but it may be the original adopted 2018-19 budget — the book does not say.
- **2017-18 actual all-funds "ALL OTHER BUDGET RESOURCES" = 422,640** (p73): 2017 GO bond issuance proceeds; explains the 1.54B actual total.
- **Contingency collapse is real, not a cut**: all-funds contingency drops 326,492 → 77,939 (76%) as 2017 bond proceeds move from contingency to programmed capital projects (p74 narrative).
- **Fund 307 (IT Projects Debt Service) zeroes out** in 2019-20 (dash columns, p126) — fund winding down. Fund 470 (Partnership) prints a dash for 2018-19 budget.
- **Contingency 77,939 vs 77,940**: program view (p74) vs account view (p75) of the same adopted all-funds contingency differ by 1k.
- **Other Capital Projects table (p160) references funds 191, 423, 424** that appear nowhere in the fund-structure summaries or appropriations — internal project-management fund designations; not extracted.
- **Tax rate presentation differs by page**: p21 prints a combined "Operations" rate of $5.2781; p176 splits it into permanent $4.7743 + gap $0.5038. The resolutions (p411, p414) impose $5.2781 + $1.9900 local option + $130,575,000 bonded debt *amount* (bond rate $2.4890 is the extended-rate estimate). $0.5038 of the permanent rate is excluded from urban renewal division-of-tax (expired Gap Tax, ORS 457.010(4)(a)(D)).
- **Bond program over original budget**: combined current project budget $1,405.5M vs $1,272M original (p159, as of 1/1/2019); book flags a planned 2020 bond campaign to complete Benson. 2017-bond EH&S lines print no original budgets.
- **Levy chart vs GF revenue**: local option levied ~104.8M (est) vs 97.35M budgeted current-year collections — heavy Measure 5 compression on the local option is expected; not an error.
- p183 note: "Beginning in Fiscal Year 2020, PPS presents position allocations in plain language" — position-title rollups changed vs prior books; FTE grand total 5,930.33 is consistent across p74, p183, p185.
- Demographic table (p178) prints a malformed first row (2007/2008 rows merged in the text layer) — not extracted.
