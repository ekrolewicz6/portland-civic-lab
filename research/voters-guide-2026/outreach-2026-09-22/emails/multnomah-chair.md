Right now the page shows 11 of 13 choices on record for you. A gap on a page is a research gap, not a position, and it says so, but readers do compare. The questions below are the gaps. A sentence or two on any of them is enough; we quote or paraphrase with a date and attribute it to your campaign, and we never fill a gap from a general goal.

Choices your published statements come close to

Choices we found no statement on

edan@portlandciviclab.org
