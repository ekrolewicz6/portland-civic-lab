Withdrawn and void initiative petitions
To qualify to the November 3, 2026, Portland ballot, a City initiative petition must contain a minimum of 40,437 valid signatures from Portland voters. This number represents nine percent of
the number of registered City voters on the date of the primary election before a petition was filed.

Signature Verification Documents
Signature Sheet Pre-Processing Results (Conducted by Portland City Elections)
During signature sheet pre-processing, Portland City Elections rejects sheets and individual signature lines as required by state election rules. Some examples of common rejection reasons may include insufficient circulator certification (issues with the signature or date), mismatching circulator signature (does not match voter registration record or exemplar provided), and use of unapproved signature sheets. The remaining signature sheets are approved for use in the next step of statistical sampling signature verification and transferred to the County.

Signature Verification Statistical Sampling Results (Conducted by Multnomah County Elections)
During the individual signature verification process, Multnomah County Elections uses the state's statistical sampling method to determine how many valid signatures are in an initiative petition. A random sample of individual signatures to review is selected by an automated system before
individual signatures are evaluated as required by state election rules. Some examples of common rejection reasons include signatures that do not match the signatures on file or signatures from ineligible signers that do not live in the City of Portland or are not registered to vote. If the first sample results in an insufficient number of valid signatures, a second sample of signatures is automatically processed.
