## Anomalies
1. Vol2 cover says "Proposed" although vol1 is the adopted book — school-report staffing there reflects the proposed stage.
2. All-funds 2021-22 total printed 2,425,370 on the resources table (p91) but 2,425,371 on the requirements tables (p92–93).
3. Fund 400 2021-22: resources total 780,617 (p155) vs requirements total 780,620 (p156). Fund 600 2023-24: resources 8,998 (p191) vs requirements 8,997 (p192). Fund 200 2020-21 appears once as 155,929 in an overlapped print (p112) vs 155,931 elsewhere. All rounding-level.
4. GF 2021-22 actuals are inflated ~$400M by the July 2021 Series 2021 pension-bond pass-through (bond proceeds 399,390 in resources p103; 400,833 in 5100/5400 requirements p100; 407,988 in object 600 p101). Distorts the 10-year GF series.
5. Extended ADMw for 2024-25: 52,213 (assumption, p26) vs 52,232 (Revenue Factors, p27) vs 52,231.82 (MESD service plan, p205).
6. 2023-24 bond tax rate: $2.38 and total $9.65 (p27) vs $2.5000 and total $9.7681 marked "Actual" (p212). Both emitted, notes flag the conflict.
7. Taxes levied before adjustment, FY2025 est.: 645,630 (p210, "Levy Amount Before Adjustment") vs 659,234 (p211, "Taxes Levied Before Adjustments") — similar concepts, different figures; likely different scope. Both pages cited; neither figure used in fragments.
8. All-funds adopted contingency 42,520 (p92) vs sum of by-fund components 42,521 (Attachment A: GF 40,856 + DS 165 + IS 1,500) — rounding.
9. Attachment A prints GF "Transfers of Funds" 15,895 (p303) vs 15,896 in the GF function/object tables (p100–101).
10. 2024-25 debt-service interest total: 65,927 (p218) vs 65,929 (p219).
11. Fund structure changes for 2024-25: two new Internal Service sub-funds — 602 Property & Liability Insurance, 603 Unemployment (p24, p190) — explain the fund-600 jump from ~9M to 25.5M. Debt-service payments table uses funds 308/322/323/350 (p219) while the TOC lists 308/320/350; 322/323 appear to be FF&C sub-funds under 320.
12. Local option levy authorization: current five-year term's final tax year is 2024-25 (ED-50 p307); renewal for a fourth term at $1.99 approved May 2024 (p22). Expect a new authorization schedule in the FY2025-26 book.
13. No Measure 98 sub-fund split in this book (that begins FY2025-26+); Student Investment Account is fund 251, Dedicated Resource 299.
14. Text layer prints most summary tables twice (chart + table overlay); values were cross-checked between the duplicate prints and against Attachment A.
