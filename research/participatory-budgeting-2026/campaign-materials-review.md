## Changes to the independent judgment

The expert letter provides direct support for this precise measure's institutional design, strengthening the YES argument beyond generic examples. The policy fact sheet and history make the durability objection to pilot-first strategies more concrete. Regional examples demonstrate useful local implementation experience. The material does **not** provide a reconciled city funding portfolio, costed staffing plan, voting rulebook, measurable goals, or evidence that all ongoing service tradeoffs disappear.

The main article retains the strongest NO case and corrects opposition overstatements about auditing, nonexistent guardrails, inevitable loss of all project dollars to services, and operating rules being unchangeable. Criticism of claim precision is applied to both campaigns.
