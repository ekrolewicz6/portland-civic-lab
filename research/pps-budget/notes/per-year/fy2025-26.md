## Anomalies
1. **Fund 252 Measure 98 - High School Success Fund "formally created as part of the District's 2025-26 budget process"** (p.137). Prior-year columns print "-" (activity previously inside Fund 205 Grants). First year of the M98 sub-fund split; adopted 2025-26 total 13,163K (p.138).
2. GF 2021-22 actuals are inflated by the 2021 pension-bond transaction: 399,390K bond proceeds in resources (p.102) and 400,833K function-5400 PERS UAL payoff in requirements (p.104). Total 1,188,261K is not comparable to adjacent years.
3. Debt total discrepancy: narrative (p.147) says outstanding debt at 6/30/2025 projected at **$1.78B**; the debt tables (p.228, p.229) print **$1,682,908K**.
4. Debt-service crosswalk: 2025-26 payments by debt service fund total 272,672 (p.229) vs Fund 300 total requirements 272,690 (p.149) vs all-funds function 5100+5400 row 272,690 (p.90).
5. Certified permanent rate $5.2781 (LB-1, resolution p.316) = $4.7743 permanent + $0.5038 gap portion (p.22); resolution excludes the $0.5038 from urban renewal division-of-tax.
6. No unappropriated/budgeted ending fund balance in 2025-26 (all funds; prior year 22,423K) to reflect PERS Rate Stabilization Reserve (Fund 225) utilization - a 23,962K transfer into the GF (p.93, p.98).
7. Internal Service Funds jump from 7,683K (2023-24 actual) to 25,509K (2024-25 budget): GF transfers 14,560K to a "602 Property & Liability Insurance" fund (p.93) that the TOC's fund list (which names only 601 and 603) does not show as a section.
8. Vol1 TOC page numbers do not match printed folios in parts of the informational section (e.g., TOC says Debt Service Overview at book p.218; printed folio on that page is 206, PDF p.225). PDF pages here were verified by pattern search, not the TOC.
9. The adopted resolution's Attachment A (p.317-318) has a garbled glyph-mapped text layer (CID font); its fund-level approved/adjustment/adopted columns are unreadable in text. Fund-level figures were taken from the clean fund-summary pages instead. The readable "Adjustment" column entries are 0.
10. Adopted-book vol2 file is byte-identical to the proposed vol2 (cover still reads "2025-26 PROPOSED BUDGET VOLUME #2") - no adoption-stage reissue of the school reports volume.
11. Rounding: GF adopted revenue rows sum to 868,582 vs printed total 868,580; object rows to 868,581 (book carries a rounding disclaimer on each table). Function rows foot exactly. All five fund-type adopted totals sum exactly to 2,035,117.
12. Extraction-integrity note: one intermediate scratchpad extract of the debt pages came back with content from a different fiscal year's book and altered page headers; it was discarded and every figure written was re-verified directly against `budget-fy2025-26-vol1.txt` by page-scoped pattern search.
