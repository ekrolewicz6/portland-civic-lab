## Positions on taxes (the answers the guide uses)

- Kotek: the tax system must be "adequate and fair"; would start on Prosperity Council ideas such as a larger research-and-development credit and adjusting the CAT for smaller businesses, but "let's not blow a giant hole in our budget," citing a $400 million hole from H.R. 1 in the Oregon Health Plan; would pay for early changes by adjusting other parts of the tax system, and later said she is open to changing the CAT threshold for small businesses, paid for by adjusting tax provisions "that haven't been that effective in making us more competitive." Wants a broader tax-reform process led by an outside party, perhaps a university, "not being led, frankly, by elected leaders."
- Drazan: "Yes, Oregon's business taxes are too high." Would raise the CAT threshold (filing starts at $750,000, the tax at $1 million) and not stop at the $2 million the Prosperity Council suggested; would send CAT money she says "fattens up" the Education Department to early reading instead; would change the estate tax; "Salem should tighten its belt just like everybody else." On local taxes: "I love local control until I don't"; local governments "should not be able to pile on" the state income tax, and she would use preemption "selectively and carefully."
