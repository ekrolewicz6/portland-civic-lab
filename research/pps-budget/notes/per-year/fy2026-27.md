## Anomalies

1. `lb1-fy2026-27.txt` contains only "Ad Content Proof" — dead fetch, needs re-pull from ODE/district.
2. Resolution Attachment A pages (319-320) have garbled font encoding in the text layer.
3. GF function 4000 (Facilities) adopted 2026-27 prints **$1K** — a placeholder line; the column only foots
   to 862,112 with it.
4. Fund 225 (PERS Rate Stabilization Reserve) drained: 2025-26 adopted moved $23,962K to the GF via
   interfund transfer (visible as the GF 5200 resource line); only $394K remains in 2026-27.
5. Fund 252 (Measure 98 – High School Success) "formally created as part of the District's 2025-26 budget
   process" (p.146) — the M98 sub-fund split; no pre-2025-26 columns.
6. Fund 602 (Property & Liability Insurance) and 603 (Unemployment) first show activity in 2024-25.
7. Rounding: GF object column for 26-27 sums to 862,113 vs printed 862,112; 2023-24 GF functions sum
   873,376 vs printed 873,375 (book carries its own rounding disclaimer).
8. All-funds contingency adopted 2025-26 = 41,679 vs GF 41,179 ($500K sat outside the GF); in 2026-27 all
   contingency (41,056) is in the GF.
9. PPS deliberately budgets reserves as **contingency, not unappropriated EFB** (p.36) — budgeted EFB = 0
   both years; actual-year reserves appear as function 7000 / object 376520.
10. Narrative typo p.36: "from $868.6 million in 2026-25" (means 2025-26).
11. AV for 2026-27: Revenue Factors (p.28) $73.56B vs TSCC estimate on p.224 $73.698B ($73,697,849K).
12. Feb 24, 2026 bond sale of $660.0M = $90M under the 2020 authorization + $570M under the May 2025
    $1.83B authorization (p.230); drives beginning fund balance to $1,418,834K and capital function 4000 to
    $1,392,645K in 2026-27.
13. All-funds "Adopted 2025-26" is the only current-year column — no Revised column in this book's
    summary tables.
14. Supt message figures (all-funds basis): closing a $56M gap; −336 FTE from 2025-26 adopted GF; >$65M
    projected deficit for 2027-28; support services $453.2M; facilities construction $1.4B (matches tables).
15. Levy: local option imposed est. 149,921 vs budgeted CY collections 103,938 — gap is compression +
    collection rate; Measure 5 adjustments printed at $53.414M for 2026-27 (p.28). Positions funded by the
    levy not quantified.
