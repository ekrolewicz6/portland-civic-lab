# Visual guide media decisions · September 27, 2026

The main guide uses a matched NASA Terra MODIS false-color view of western Oregon from July 19 and September 27, 2020 as its cover and repeats the full images in the aftermath chapter. Images are already versioned in `public/images/oregon-fire`. Caption states dates, extent, product and publisher; it does not treat every dark pixel as a measured severity class. Source: https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/gallery/individual.php?db_date=2020-09-29 .

Two publisher-hosted videos now appear in the chapters they support. Both use click-to-load embeds, have direct publisher links, and are optional to the written story. The embedded films and publisher thumbnails are not copied into the repository.

The diagrams explain process and explicitly label the compared conditions. The four landscape cards identify different *questions*, not four exhaustive Oregon zones or a prescription. Continue seeking source-cleared photographs of actual Oregon projects and dated local repeat photography; do not use a Colorado Plateau or Utah comparison as Oregon evidence.
