# Medicaid work-rules card: sources (September 28, 2026)

Prompted by the Braver Angels review: the card gave Oregon's coverage-loss estimate but not the rules' stated purpose or the federal savings, and cited OPB.

## CBO supplemental cost estimate, P.L. 119-21, Title VII, Subtitle B, Chapter 1 (Medicaid)
- Page: https://www.cbo.gov/publication/61837 (PDF: https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2025-10/PL-119-21-Medicaid%20_0.pdf), October 28, 2025. cbo.gov returns 403 to scripted fetches; read in the browser pane.
- Section 71119 "directs states to require certain Medicaid enrollees to spend at least 80 hours each month in activities, such as work, job training, education, or community service, as a condition of eligibility."
- "most Medicaid enrollees who are subject to the requirement either will satisfy it or will qualify for an exception; the rest, about 2.9 million in 2034, will lose coverage." About 2.8 million more lose coverage because of the added verification steps.
- "CBO and JCT estimate that implementing section 71119 will decrease deficits by $317.0 billion over the 2025-2034 period. CBO also estimates that the number of people without health insurance will increase by 5.3 million in 2034."
- Whole Medicaid chapter: $886.8 billion deficit reduction over 2025–2034; 7.5 million more uninsured in 2034.

## CMS on the purpose
- Press release, June 1, 2026, "CMS Launches Nationwide Framework to Implement Medicaid Work Requirements": https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-launches-nationwide-framework-implement-medicaid-work-requirements
- "The rule establishes a nationwide operational framework designed to promote economic stability, self-sufficiency, and independence."
- Administrator Oz: "This rule helps Americans build skills and independence through work, education, job training, or community service, creating new opportunities for themselves and their families."
- The release cites an HHS/ASPE study saying the requirements "could reduce poverty by as much as 2.9 million people depending on a variety of conditions such as employment availability" (not used on the card).
- Fact sheet (CMS-2454-IFC, June 1, 2026): https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicaid-community-engagement-requirement-certain-individuals-interim-final-rule-comment-period-cms ($580 a month = 80 × federal minimum wage; states must implement by January 1, 2027).

## Oregon figures
- The ~600,000 adults to be checked, 100,000–200,000 possible coverage losses and $718 million to $1.4 billion a year in federal funds come from OHA officials as reported by OPB (July 29, 2026) and Willamette Week (September 14, 2026). OPB (September 22, 2026) quotes Interim Medicaid Director Vivian Levy: OHA must notify "a little less than half" of 1.4 million OHP members.
