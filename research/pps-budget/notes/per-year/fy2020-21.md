Source: `budget-fy2020-21-vol1` (326 PDF pages), `budget-fy2020-21-vol2` (175 PDF pages).
First two-volume year. Vol2 is Individual School Reports only (per-school enrollment,
FTE, demographics) — no fund schedules; nothing was extracted from it.
All page numbers below are 1-indexed PDF pages of the .txt (form-feed delimited).
Printed page = PDF page − 7 through the main body.

## Column → basis mapping used

Seven-column fund tables: Actual 2016-17/2017-18/2018-19 → `actual-budgetary`;
Budget 2019-20 → `revised`; Proposed/Approved/Adopted 2020-21 → `proposed`/`approved`/`adopted`.

## Anomalies

1. **$1.0B bond placeholder added at approval.** Proposed total was $1,725,777K; the
   Approved/Adopted total is $2,725,777K after a $1,000,000K "placeholder for a possible
   Bond Measure in 2020-21" was added (All Other Budget Resources 84 → 1,000,084;
   Fund 450 GO Bonds 552,542 → 1,552,542; Attachment A shows it as a $1.0B
   "Adjustment" to Capital Project Funds, p.320). The adopted all-funds total is
   therefore not comparable to prior adopted years without backing this out.
2. **New fund: 251 Student Investment Account** (Student Success Act / SIA), first
   budgeted 2020-21 at $39,162K; all prior-year columns printed as dashes.
3. **Fund 307 IT Projects Debt Service** shows dashes from 2019-20 onward (wound down);
   **Fund 407 IT System Project** drops to dashes in 2020-21 (had $8K budget 2019-20).
4. **GF detail total does not foot with summary**: GF requirements detail (p.122, p.125)
   prints 691,769 for the 2019-20 Budget column vs 691,767 in the summary tables
   (p.117–119). Book carries a blanket rounding disclaimer; summaries used.
5. **Leaf-fund sums vs printed all-funds totals** differ by ±2K in every year-column
   (rounding of thousands on the per-fund pages).
6. **2017 bond table header glitch** (p.221): the fourth column is headed "Actuals
   Approved" twice; by position and the 2012 section it is the Budget FY21 column.
   Only Original/Current/Actuals columns were extracted.
7. **Gap tax folded into permanent rate**: resolution (p.319) imposes a single $5.2781
   operations rate and separately resolves that $0.5038 of it (the expired Gap Levy
   portion) is excluded from urban renewal division-of-tax. p.238 still prints
   permanent 4.7743 and gap 0.5038 as separate rates; tax_rates.csv keeps them split.
8. **Proposed → Approved GF object shifts**: 300 Purchased Services 92,497 → 92,322 and
   400 Supplies 18,489 → 18,664 (offsetting, total unchanged). All other GF lines
   identical across Proposed/Approved/Adopted.
9. **Bonded debt levy imposed as an amount, not a rate**: $136,000,000 for exempt bonds
   (p.319); the $2.45 bond rate (p.53/p.238) is a projection ("actual levy rate
   determined by county assessors' offices").
10. Post Budget Adoption Update (printed p.3): book notes COVID-era update; the
    April 14, 2020 sale of $441.3M Series 2020B GO bonds landed between proposal and
    adoption (p.243) — outstanding-debt figures are "@ June 30, 2020" and include it.
