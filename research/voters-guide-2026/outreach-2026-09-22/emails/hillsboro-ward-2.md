Subject: Portland Civic Lab voters guide: Ivette Pantoja, Hillsboro Council, Ward 2, Position A

Hi Ivette,

I run Portland Civic Lab, a nonpartisan, volunteer research project publishing a voters guide for every race on the November 3 ballot. Your page is at https://www.portlandciviclab.org/voters-guide/hillsboro-ward-2/ivette-pantoja. It is built only from your published words (your filed statement, your site and public statements) and the public record, the same way for every candidate, with a source on every line. It carries no endorsements, rankings or scores. The race page also has a board for each live choice the City of Hillsboro faces, with every candidate’s statement or a gap: https://www.portlandciviclab.org/voters-guide/hillsboro-ward-2#topics.

Right now the page shows 1 of 6 choices on record for you. A gap on a page is a research gap, not a position, and it says so, but readers do compare. The questions below are the gaps. A sentence or two on any of them is enough; we quote or paraphrase with a date and attribute it to your campaign, and we never fill a gap from a general goal.

The four issues every race page tracks
1. Rent and homes. Your page shows “Missing-middle homes.” We could not find how you would deliver it (the funding source, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partner), or what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.
2. Camps, crime and who responds. What balance of enforcement, prevention and care do you propose?
3. Your bills and taxes. Your page shows “Jobs and apprenticeships.” We could not find what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.
4. Streets, buses and air. Where would you invest, and what would you restrict?

Choices your published statements come close to
5. Housing and displacement. Add local tenant protections and a downtown anti-displacement strategy, or focus on loosening rules to build more? (Your page carries “Missing-middle to multifamily” from your published statement; it does not say which way you come down on this choice.)

Choices we found no statement on
6. Data-center moratorium. Adopt the new data-center zoning limits on October 6 and extend the 120-day moratorium past November 24?
7. Data-center tax breaks. Raise the fees data centers pay on abated taxes and set stricter terms on new abatements?
8. Utility fees. Raise the transportation utility fee to $11.64 a month and sewer and stormwater rates about 5% from January 1?
9. Human Rights Office. Fund a Human Rights Office ($321,000–$553,000 a year) and more legal aid on top of the sanctuary ordinance?
10. Private-jet hangars. Approve Sky Harbour’s seven private-jet hangars at the airport when it refiles?

If any line on your page is wrong, out of date or unfair, say so and I will correct it and note the change in the research log. Anything you send on the record we treat the same as we treat every campaign’s statements. Thanks for your time.

Edan Krolewicz
Portland Civic Lab
edan@portlandciviclab.org
