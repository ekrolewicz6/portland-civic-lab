Nothing here has been sent to a campaign.

## Least sure

1. **Brim-Edwards / HSD oversight, "supports."** The quote is explicit, but the cell's framing as support for tighter monitoring rests on her Sunstone statement plus the media PDF's pledge to implement the auditor's recommendations. Her yes vote on VerGow is inferred by elimination from KPTV's 3–2 roll on a five-member Board (Singleton and Jones-Dixon named as the no votes); the county's April 14 release gives no roll. The Board minutes for April 9, 2026 would settle it; they were not read.
2. **Meieran / Preschool tax delay, "partial."** Podcast speech, read from auto-captions: "more into preschool and at this point I would say not, um, more into something else. Do we raise taxes like we said we would even though we have more than we bargained for." The sentence is a question she poses, not an answer; a reviewer may prefer "gap."
3. **Meieran / Moda, "partial."** Same podcast; she says she "can't speak to where each million... would go." The cell records her framing (economic decision, negotiate, no reset), not a yes or no.
4. **Meieran / Sobering, "partial."** The only direct quote is from April 2024, before the current center was sited; her 2026 behavioral-health plan is a Google Doc linked from fix-multco.com, not a sharonforchair.com page.
5. **Singleton / Jail capacity, "partial."** A whole-budget vote; she has never spoken to jail capacity itself. Could be coded "gap."
6. **Singleton / HSD oversight, "partial."** KPTV gives her reason for the VerGow no vote (wait for the chair election) in the reporter's words, not a quote.
7. **Brim-Edwards and Singleton / Homeless-services tax, "partial."** Both quotes are from January 2025 on Metro's earlier draft, twenty months old; neither has addressed the 2028 plan.
8. **Sheriff / Sanctuary, "partial."** The words are the office's (communications director; chief of staff), issued in her name, framed as compliance with Oregon law rather than a choice; the OregonLive story of June 24 (blocked) may carry a direct quote.
9. **Sheriff / Deflection, "supports."** The letter is unambiguous on more support for the center; it says nothing about the "tighten" half (90-day completion rules).
10. **Ong Norris / Sanctuary, "gap."** "We are resisting authoritarian attacks on our immigrant neighbors" (pamphlet) was left out as a value statement; a reviewer could read it as partial.
11. **Cruz partials (four).** Her issues page is live and dated September 22, 2026; the answers are broad. All four cells name what is unsaid; none should be read as support for the specific choice.
12. **Coverage limits.** Every OregonLive, KGW and most KOIN pages were unreadable; the AOKTV forum (57 minutes, all three chair candidates, August 27, 2026) has no transcript; the WebSearch budget ran out partway through the District 2 sweep, so news coverage for District 2 was searched mainly through Google News RSS. The Mother PAC forum on October 15 will be the first venue with all ten chair and District 2 candidates answering the same questions.
