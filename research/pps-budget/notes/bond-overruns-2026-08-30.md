# Bond overruns: causes, base rates, peers (research pass, 2026-08-30)

Agent research pass answering: what causes the PPS construction overruns, in what proportions,
and are they Portland-specific? Feeds the "anatomy of an overrun" exhibit in the waste section.
Corpus side: `notes/per-year/bonds.md` (Benson waterfall, auditor-decomposed Mar 2022 step).

## Base rate: overruns are the norm everywhere
- Flyvbjerg (Oxford), 16,000+ projects, 136 countries: 91.5% miss budget or schedule; constant
  ~70 years. Real overrun means: rail +45%, bridges/tunnels +34%, roads +20%; >50% "not
  uncommon," fat tail. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.2176 · https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.7400 ·
  https://medium.com/data-science/the-iron-law-of-megaprojects-18b886590f0b
- **No national K-12 school-bond overrun base-rate study exists** — verified absence; state-level
  only (CA Getting Down to Facts: https://gettingdowntofacts.com/sites/default/files/2018-09/GDTFII_Report_Brunner-Vincent.pdf).

## Peers
- **Seattle** (BEX): overruns absorbed inside rolling levies (Rainier Beach $206.5M constr →
  $276.3M total across BEX V + BTA V: https://www.seattletimes.com/education-lab/new-high-school-could-be-a-crown-jewel-for-rainier-beach/);
  pattern old (Ballard 1996 ~$8M over). Structural difference: 6-yr levies + program contingency
  + follow-on levy every ~3 yrs.
- **Beaverton**: 2014 $680M bond's on-budget reputation rests on district-published material
  (https://bond.beaverton.k12.or.us/2014-2022-bond-program) — no independent wholesale audit
  found; label "per district reporting." 2022 bond's $253M Beaverton HS reported on budget
  (Beaverton Valley Times 2025-03-13). $12M rolled 2014→Raleigh Hills.
- **SFUSD 2016 $744M bond**: verified mess — seven promised projects need new bond money;
  Buena Vista Horace Mann $40M (2022) → $105M+$7.5M. https://thefrisc.com/schools-eye-a-1-billion-bond-sfs-biggest-ever-while-questions-persist-about-the-last-one-7dbc1ea0b9bf/ ·
  https://www.sfpublicpress.org/officials-vow-to-fix-crumbling-school-we-have-failed-you/
- **LAUSD**: Belmont $175M halted (env.); IG found 50% cost exceedances, $31M unapproved
  contracts. https://bondoversight.lausd.org/

## Market escalation 2020-2023 (named index)
- **Turner Building Cost Index**: 2021 +1.9%, 2022 +8.0%, 2023 +6.0% ≈ **+17% cumulative
  2020→2023**. https://turnerconstruction.com/uploads/CostIndex2023Qtr1.pdf ·
  https://www.turnerconstruction.com/cost-index. (Mortenson 2021 +26% per WA DES compilation —
  secondhand, don't cite alone: https://des.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2025-09/2025-08-20-CCE-Comm-CCI-Comparisons-MRSC.pdf)
- Implication: market explains a fraction of Benson's +108%.

## PPS-specific causes (public record)
- **$100M pre-ballot cut audit-verified**: OSM leadership cut ~$100M from staff/professional
  estimates before the 2017 ballot "without a documented method, rationale, or explanation";
  auditors couldn't identify the decider; board members linked to polling ($750M polled better
  than $850M). https://www.opb.org/news/article/portland-public-schools-bond-lowered/ · +37%
  within ~1 yr: https://www.opb.org/news/article/portland-oregon-schools-budget-vote/
- 2018 BAC: $190M unfunded gap across 4 schools (KATU: https://katu.com/news/local/report-shows-portland-public-schools-is-200-million-overbudget-for-work-within-2017-bond).
- Benson: $202M→$269M (2018)→$410M+; **PPS v. Andersen Construction litigation over who owes**
  (https://www.constructionowners.com/news/pps-andersen-clash-over-benson-high-costs).
- Jefferson: $311M→$366M→$491M (Apr 2024)→$465M post cost-reduction
  (https://www.opb.org/article/2024/04/09/portland-jefferson-high-school-rebuild-costs-increase/);
  OPB's partial itemization (+$16M structures, +$17.7M interiors, +$25M electrical, ~$44M
  unexplained) is the closest public decomposition anywhere.
- Annual performance audits (Sjoberg Evashenk — NOTE: auditor of record; FY23/24 audit, 23
  recommendations, WW 2025-06-29) do NOT decompose causes by percentage.
- **No published X%-inflation / Y%-scope / Z%-error decomposition of PPS overruns exists** —
  the corpus Benson Mar-2022 step (+21.2 escalation / +17 scope / +14 budgeting error of a
  $52.4M jump, bond audit) is the only quantified step.
- Counterexample: Lincoln HS $245M on time/on budget, same district, same market (KGW).
- Teacher unions: no connection (bond money legally capital-only; no reporting ties PLAs or
  labor terms to the overruns; prevailing wage is statewide baseline). The 2025 controversy was
  a $61.5M bond-management contract, not labor
  (https://www.opb.org/article/2025/12/03/portland-school-board-approves-contract-bond-projects/).
