# FY2022-23 extraction worksheet

Corpus: `budget-fy2022-23-vol1.txt` (364 PDF pages), `budget-fy2022-23-vol2.txt` (180 PDF pages).
Vol2 is Individual School Reports only — no district-level financial tables; every extracted figure
comes from vol1. Printed page = PDF page − 7 throughout vol1.

**Adopted totals as printed:** All Funds $1,883,261K (Resolution 6520, PDF p356: "the total amount
of $1,883,261,000"; summary tables PDF p140-142). General Fund $804,062K (PDF p149-151;
Attachment A PDF p358).

## Year-columns in the multi-year tables

Every summary and fund-detail table carries: Actual 2018-19, Actual 2019-20, Actual 2020-21,
Budget 2021-22 (+ Budget FTE 2021-22), Proposed 2022-23, Approved 2022-23, Adopted 2022-23
(+ Budget FTE 2022-23). The 2021-22 column is labeled only "Budget"; it was emitted as
basis=adopted (the FY2021-22 adopted budget as carried in this book). A row was emitted per
year-column in every fragment.

Approval trail as printed (PDF p355, p358): Proposed 1,868,806 → +18,510 adjustments →
Approved 1,887,316 → −4,055 amendment (GO Bonds Debt Service UEFB) → Adopted 1,883,261.
GF: Proposed 794,585 → +9,477 → Approved/Adopted 804,062.

## Anomalies

1. **Attachment A fund mislabel (p358):** the Internal Service Funds section is headed
   "600 - Capital Project Funds"; its total (8,646) matches the Internal Service Funds summary.
2. **Adoption amendment:** GO Bonds Debt Service Fund (350) UEFB cut 4,055K between approved
   and adopted (163,418 → 159,363), the only approved→adopted change (p358).
3. **TSCC recommendation (p362):** GO Bond Debt Fund taxes budgeted at ~$149M vs the
   ORS 294.368 calculation of ~$145M; TSCC recommended following the required calculation.
   The TSCC certification page (p363) splits the approved budget into six funds, including
   Debt Service 83,047K + GO Bonds Debt Service 163,418K.
4. **GF Support Services proposed discrepancy:** the GF function summary (p150) and Attachment A
   (p358) print 2000 proposed as 339,250, but the GF minor-function detail subtotal (p153)
   prints 337,875 — an unexplained 1,375K gap in the proposed column only (approved/adopted
   agree at 341,772).
5. **p272 tax-rate table 2021-22 column** (headed "Actual") repeats the 2018-19 stack
   (bond 2.4890, total 9.7571), while p34 prints 2021-22 total 9.60 (bond ≈2.33) and the
   FY2023-24 book prints 2.3335. Recorded as printed with a warning note.
6. **AV disagreement:** 2022-23 assessed value $62.60B on p34 vs $62,725,000K (est.) on p270.
7. **Levy tables disagree:** p270 "Levy Amount Before Adjustments" FY2023 = 612,705K vs p271
   "Taxes Levied Before Adjustments" 2023 = 580,549K (scopes not reconciled in the book).
8. **Forecast typo (p42):** all-funds 3-year forecast prints adopted Budgeted Ending Fund
   Balance 46,563 vs 46,536 everywhere else (digit transposition).
9. **Rounding seams (recorded in row notes):** all-funds 2020-21 resources 2,233,098 vs
   requirements 2,233,100; GF 2019-20 object-table total 709,091 vs 709,092; BFB adopted
   663,328 vs 663,327; DS 2019-20 191,558/191,557; Capital 2018-19 498,977/498,976 and
   Attachment A adopted capital 516,865 vs 516,864; fund 404 2020-21 14,679/14,678;
   fund 420 2018-19 5,822/5,821; fund 601 vs group-600 totals differ by 1K in 2018-19/2019-20;
   proposed contingency 59,248 (p141) vs 59,247 (p142).
10. **FTE table label (pp281-282):** rightmost column is headed "2023 Proposed" but its grand
    total (6,529.74) equals the adopted-budget FTE printed on p141; emitted as proposed with note.
11. **Fund lifecycle:** Student Investment Account Fund (251) first active 2020-21; IT Projects
    Debt Service Fund (307) last active 2018-19; IT System Project Fund (407) and Partnership
    Funds (470) last active 2019-20. High School Success (M98) money sits inside Grants Fund
    (205) — the M98 sub-fund split does not exist until FY2025-26.
12. **Restatements to expect:** the FY2023-24 book restates 2020-21 ADMw as 55,688 and 2021-22
    as ADMr 44,747 / ADMw 53,398, vs 55,904 and 45,354 / 54,241 printed here (this book's
    figures were emitted as printed).
13. The text layer double-renders most summary tables (overlapping duplicate prints); values
    were taken from the clean single-line renders and cross-footed (sub-funds vs group totals
    vs all-funds total, and GF line items vs totals, all within 2K rounding).
