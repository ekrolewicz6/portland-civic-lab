# About panel and site sharing refresh — September 19, 2026

The race About view now opens with an introduction, election facts and three
clear routes into candidate profiles, comparisons and the Council record.
Council powers, candidate differences, all seven glossary definitions, source
links and research limitations remain available. The incomplete independent
human-review disclosure remains visible. The design was checked at 320, 390,
768 and 1365 pixels; keyboard navigation focuses the heading rather than drawing
a browser outline around the entire panel. Interactive controls keep their
visible focus styles.

The homepage now identifies the Lab as a source of voter guides, city data and
research in its title and search/social descriptions. The previous dashboard-only
image and changing edition date have been replaced with an illustrated Portland
bridge and the message “Understand Portland.” Other major pages missing complete
social metadata now use the shared page metadata helper, including the dashboard,
city performance, directory, contact, support and concierge pages. The unpublished
progress-report archive retains its no-index setting.

The shared image system uses bundled Cormorant and DM Sans fonts, cream and green
panels, large titles, and decorative illustrations for city life, data, research,
people and places. The drawings are not geographic maps or statistical charts.
Existing quantitative callouts remain separate from the decorative artwork.
Long titles are sized conservatively by estimated word wrapping; major landing
page titles were also shortened to identify their subject at thumbnail size.
The existing Oregon Fire and voter-guide artwork retain their dedicated designs.

Verification includes raw social-crawler HTML, canonical and social URLs,
matching descriptions, image alt text, Twitter large-image tags, PNG decoding,
dimensions and image size. Both Edge and Node image routes are exercised.
Forty public static cards were rendered for visual comparison, including the
unchanged Fire atlas. Both race About views, their links and disclosures, and
existing voter-guide sharing/navigation were checked in the production build.
These checks do not clear social-platform caches or guarantee search ranking.
