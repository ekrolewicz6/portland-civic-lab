## Answers (WHAT / HOW / AS MEASURED BY), verbatim in the email; summarized here with how each was used
1. Housing and homelessness: production and preservation; cut unnecessary development barriers, modernize the affordable-housing funding formula, reuse properties, align state funding with local plans, coordinate resident services. Measured by homes for low-income households, development time and cost, moves into permanent housing, housing stability. Used for his housing column and ladder.
2. Crime, drugs and who responds: coordinated enforcement, legal frameworks, specialized resources "to protect residents from ICE apprehension," treatment, prevention and accountability for drug use. Measured by reduced ICE apprehensions, repeat offenses, treatment engagement, response times. Used for his safety column and ladder.
3. Taxes, kicker, budget: evaluate the EITC, protect the kicker, make large energy users pay their own costs. Used for his money column and ladder (replacing "Measurable results" from the Ballotpedia survey).
4. Roads, transit, climate: preservation first, targeted safety, transit investments with lifecycle-cost analysis, sustainable funding and reduced climate impact. Measured by pavement and bridge condition, crashes, transit access and ridership, delivery on budget. Used for his climate column and ladder.
5. 2027 road package: "without committing taxpayers to another increase before its necessity and impacts are demonstrated." Board: partial.
6. Kicker: "Examine existing reserves, dedicated revenues, and alternative funding before considering any proposal to retain future kicker funds." Board: partial (does not rule it out).
7. Budget gap: review program effectiveness, costs, obligations "and revenue options before adopting a complete funding package." Board: partial (no choice between revenue and cuts).
8. Data centers: separate large-user utility rates, land-use and public-benefit requirements, review Enterprise Zone incentives before new abatements; "As demonstrated by the City of Forest Grove." Board: supports. The Forest Grove claim is kept only in his verbatim answer on his page.
9. Shelter funding after June 2027: "Sustain effective shelter and housing-stability services." Board: supports (the emergency declaration is not addressed).
10. Deflection: "Fund county implementation, establish workable statewide standards." Board: supports.
11. Wildfire: "Strengthen wildfire prevention, community preparedness, and firefighting capacity through sustainable funding." Board: supports.
12. Sanctuary limits: compliance with Oregon law, clear agency responsibilities, training, indemnification, deployment protocols; measured partly by reduced ICE apprehensions. Board: partial (does not say whether to add limits).
