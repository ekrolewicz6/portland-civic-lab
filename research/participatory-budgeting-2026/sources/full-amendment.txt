Section  2-129  Participatory  Budgeting  Program  
To  further  public  engagement  and  democratic  involvement  in  City  spending,  the  City  must  create  by  
ordinance
 
a
 
Participatory
 
Budgeting
 
Program
 
with
 
an
 
annual
 
or
 
bi-annual
 
participatory
 
budgeting
 
(PB)
 
process
 
open
 
to
 
all
 
residents,
 
consistent
 
with
 
the
 
Oregon
 
Local
 
Budget
 
Law.
 
Annual
 
funding
 
for
 
the
 
PB
 
Program
 
must
 
be
 
in
 
an
 
amount
 
equivalent
 
to
 
at
 
least
 
2%
 
of
 
the
 
City’s
 
adopted
 
General
 
Fund
 
discretionary
 
ongoing
 
expenses
 
from
 
the
 
previous
 
fiscal
 
year,
 
with
 
the
 
City
 
Council
 
authorized
 
to
 
identify
 
funding
 
sources
 
from
 
the
 
entire
 
City
 
budget
 
to
 
meet
 
that
 
obligation.
    
Funding
 
allocation
 
decisions
 
made
 
by
 
city
 
residents
 
through
 
the
 
PB
 
process
 
defined
 
in
 
this
 
Section
 
shall
 
be
 
binding
 
on
 
the
 
City.
 
Program
 
funding
 
shall
 
cover
 
staffing
 
and
 
initial
 
and
 
ongoing
 
PB
 
process
 
implementation
 
and
 
evaluation.
 
The
 
PB
 
Program
 
funding
 
shall
 
begin
 
no
 
later
 
than
 
the
 
2027-2028
 
fiscal
 
year
 
and
 
the
 
first
 
PB
 
Process
 
shall
 
begin
 
no
 
later
 
than
 
July
 
2028.
 
Participatory  budgeting  (PB)  is  a  deliberative  democratic  process  that  allows  residents  to  decide,  within  
certain
 
parameters,
 
how
 
to
 
spend
 
a
 
portion
 
of
 
a
 
public
 
budget.
 
The
 
Participatory
 
Budgeting
 
Program
 
(PB)
 
created
 
by
 
ordinance
 
must
 
include
 
a
 
PB
 
process
 
with
 
the
 
following
 
elements:
 
1.
     
 
Participatory  Budgeting  Oversight  Committee.  A  PB  Oversight  Committee  that  includes,  but  
is
 
not
 
limited
 
to,
 
residents
 
representing
 
all
 
City
 
Council
 
districts
 
shall
 
be
 
selected
 
by
 
a
 
process
 
determined
 
by
 
the
 
City
 
Council.
 
The
 
PB
 
Oversight
 
Committee
 
shall
 
provide
 
program
 
oversight
 
and
 
inform
 
staff
 
implementation
 
of
 
each
 
stage
 
of
 
the
 
PB
 
process,
 
with
 
an
 
emphasis
 
on
 
making
 
the
 
process
 
cost-effective,
 
transparent
 
and
 
accessible
 
to
 
all
 
City
 
residents.
 
The
 
PB
 
Oversight
 
Committee
 
will
 
not
 
make
 
decisions
 
about
 
how
 
funds
 
allocated
 
through
 
the
 
Participatory
 
Budgeting
 
process
 
are
 
spent.
 
2.
     
 
Idea  Collection.  All  residents  of  the  City  are  invited  and  encouraged  to  submit  project  ideas  on  
how
 
to
 
spend
 
available
 
funds
 
through
 
public
 
meetings
 
and
 
other
 
forums
 
that
 
are
 
open
 
and
 
accessible
 
to
 
all
 
residents.
 
3.
     
 
Project  Development.  A  transparent  process  wherein  City  staff  and  residents,  represented  by  
budget
 
delegates,
  
review,
 
assess,
 
and
 
refine
 
project
 
ideas
 
into
 
a
 
limited
 
number
 
of
 
PB
 
qualified
 
projects
 
that
 
can
 
be
 
feasibly
 
implemented
 
by
 
City
 
government.
 
PB
 
qualified
 
projects
 
can
 
be
 
specific
 
to
 
a
 
city
 
council
 
district
 
or
 
apply
 
city
 
wide.
 
4.
     
 
Project  Selection.  The  PB  qualified  projects  are  placed  on  a  ballot  and  subject  to  a  binding  
selection
 
process
 
open
 
and
 
accessible
 
to
 
all
 
residents.
 
The
 
project
 
selection
 
process
 
shall
 
be
 
developed
 
and
 
administered
 
by
 
the
 
PB
 
Program.
 
Ballots
 
may
 
include
 
both
 
citywide
 
and
 
city
 
council
 
district-specific
 
projects.
 
This
 
binding
 
selection
 
process
 
is
 
distinct
 
from
 
and
 
not
 
subject
 
to
 
city
 
or
 
state
 
laws
 
governing
 
elections
 
and
 
voting.
 
5.
     
 
Project  Implementation.  The  winning  PB  projects  are  implemented  by  City  government  or  its  
agents.
 
To
 
account
 
for
 
unforeseen
 
circumstances,
 
the
 
PB
 
program
 
can
 
include
 
a
 
limited
 
process
 
for
 
potentially
 
amending
 
winning
 
PB
 
projects
 
after
 
the
 
vote,
 
but
 
only
 
to
 
ensure
 
the
 
feasibility
 
and
 
the
 
intent
 
of
 
residents.
 
City
 
staff
 
shall
 
consult
 
the
 
PB
 
Oversight
 
Committee
 
in
 
making
 
any
 
changes
 
to
 
winning
 
PB
 
projects.
 
6.
     
 
Program  Evaluation.  The  PB  Oversight  Committee  and  City  staff  shall  evaluate  the  PB  
program
 
and
 
recommend
 
changes
 
in
 
the
 
process
 
to
 
the
 
City
 
Administrator
 
and
 
City
 
Council.
 
Evaluations
 
will
 
aim
 
to
 
improve
 
desired
 
impacts
 
and
 
outcomes
 
and
 
to
 
increase
 
participation
 
of
 
all
 
residents
 
in
 
all
 
phases
 
of
 
the
 
process.
 
In
 
addition,
 
the
 
City
 
Auditor
 
shall
 
periodically
 
conduct
 
an
 
independent
 
performance
 
audit
 
of
 
the
 
Participating
 
Budgeting
 
Program.
