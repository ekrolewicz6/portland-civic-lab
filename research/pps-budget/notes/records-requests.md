**Nothing here has been sent. Edan decides what goes out and when.** Legal
frame: Oregon Public Records Law, ORS 192.311-192.478. Agencies must
acknowledge within five business days (ORS 192.324). Fee waivers are available
where disclosure primarily benefits the public. Denials by local agencies are
appealable to the Multnomah County District Attorney.

ODE asks start informal (their sites invite it).

> quarterly) financial reports, budget monitoring reports, or budget-to-actual
