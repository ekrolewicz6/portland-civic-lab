# What was sent (September 22, 2026)

The follow-up emails in this folder went out from edan@portlandciviclab.org on the evening of September 22, 2026, between 5:37 and 7:28 PM PDT, one at a time, about a minute apart. 114 were sent and none failed. The per-message ledger (recipient, Gmail message and thread ids, send time) is private and stays in `runtime-data/voters-guide-2026/outreach/2026-09-22-final-results.jsonl`.

## How they were sent

- 85 drafts were rebuilt before sending from the data as it stood that evening (the Sollitt and Green changes had landed since the drafts were made), 26 went out as drafted, and 3 Council replies (Kimberly Tucker, Martin Ward, Mitch Green) were sent as fresh replies in their September 19 threads, because editing a reply draft pulls it out of its thread.
- The eight scheduled send tasks set up first did not send anything: a scheduled session cannot answer a permission prompt, so each one stalled at its first send. The sends ran from an attended session instead, paced about a minute apart.
- The Gmail connector rewrites any `https://` or lowercase-domain link into a `google.com/url` redirect when it writes a draft or sends. Every link in these emails is written as `PortlandCivicLab.org/voters-guide/...`, which survives and still auto-links, and a test send was checked raw before the batch.
