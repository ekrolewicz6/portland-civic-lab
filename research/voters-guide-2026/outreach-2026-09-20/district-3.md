## Paste block: what the ladder is

> Our race page shows each documented position as a three-rung ladder: **what** you would do, **how** you would deliver it (the money, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partnership), and **measured by** (the number, result or date that would show it worked). We fill the second and third rungs only from your own words. Where your materials do not yet say, the page shows a gap and, once we have asked you, says “asked.” The questions below name exactly those gaps. A sentence or two per question is enough; we quote or paraphrase with attribution and link the reply. We also list a few yes/no questions on specific choices Council has faced or will face; we only record a stance when a candidate has stated one, never by inference.
