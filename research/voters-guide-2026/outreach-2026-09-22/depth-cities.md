# Depth pass: the fourteen city races outside Portland City Council (September 22, 2026)

Scope: Portland City Auditor and Gresham (city-multnomah.ts); Beaverton, Hillsboro and Tigard (city-washington.ts);
Lake Oswego and Oregon City (city-clackamas.ts). Each pack now carries `topics` (one `RaceTopics` per city, ids
prefixed `aud-`, `gresham-`, `beaverton-`, `hillsboro-`, `tigard-`, `lo-`, `oc-`), `topicStances` and one `RaceStakes`
per race. Reviewed fields: reviewedOn 2026-09-22, reviewedBy pending.

Method. Live choices were taken from the city's own record first (agendas, minutes, adopted budgets, ordinances,
official releases) and dated; a candidate gets a cell only for an explicit statement on that choice (their site,
pamphlet statement, a questionnaire, a quote printed by a named outlet) or, for a sitting official, a recorded vote
or official action. Gaps are gaps. The web-search budget for this session ran out early, so research went through
direct fetches of city, county and campaign pages, cached page text from the September 21 sweep, and outlet pages
reached by URL; where a venue could not be reached it is listed as unchecked below.

Budget sizes in the stakes intros reuse the figures verified for the hub ladder on September 21, 2026
(src/lib/voters-guide/race-sheet/scale.ts) and were re-read on the city pages cited there.
