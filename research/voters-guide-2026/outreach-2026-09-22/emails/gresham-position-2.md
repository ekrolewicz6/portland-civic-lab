4. Streets, buses and air. Your page shows “Green responsible growth.” We could not find how you would deliver it (the funding source, rule change, staffing, sequencing or partner), or what number, deadline or visible result would show it worked.

Choices your published statements come close to

6. Groundwater and rates. Stay on the new groundwater system and fix the taste and hardness complaints, with water rates rising 5% a year from 2028? (Your page carries “Smart move on wells” from your published statement; it does not say which way you come down on this choice.)

Choices we found no statement on

8. General-fund gap. Keep drawing reserves to cover a $10 million general-fund gap, or cut services or raise revenue?
