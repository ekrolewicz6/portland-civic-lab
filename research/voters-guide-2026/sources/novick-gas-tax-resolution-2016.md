# Steve Novick: 2016 city gas tax referral (Resolution 37185)

Supports the source added to Steve Novick's District 3 brief on October 7, 2026, for the accomplishment he lists as "Passed gas tax" (his emailed answer of September 21, 2026).

## The record

- City of Portland Resolution 37185, "As Amended": refer a measure to City voters for the May 17, 2016 election authorizing a program dedicated to street repair and traffic safety through a temporary, ten-cents per gallon tax on motor vehicle fuels in Portland for vehicles not subject to weight-mile tax, and create City Code 17.105.
- City Auditor archive (eFiles), record 8519330: https://efiles.portlandoregon.gov/record/8519330/file/document/ (5 pages; SHA-256 a608b154b8768372c8f8…, full hash below). Related records in the same file (D/78896): exhibits 8519331, additional documents 8519332, presentation 8519333, testimony 8519334.
- The campaign emailed the same PDF on October 7, 2026 after a volunteer located it. Byte-for-byte identical to the eFiles copy (same size, 2,235,125 bytes, and same SHA-256).

## What it establishes

- Cover sheet (page 5): "Introduced by Commissioner Steve Novick"; filed with the Auditor January 19, 2016; Council meeting January 27, 2016, time certain 2:00 PM; prepared by Mark Lear (PBOT), January 14, 2016; Commissioners voted yes: Fritz, Fish, Saltzman, Novick, Hales (5–0, no nays).
- Page 4: "Adopted by the Council, JAN 27 2016"; "Commissioner Steve Novick" as sponsor.
- Pages 1–3: the tax was estimated to raise $64 million over four years, to be collected no earlier than September 2016 and to expire four years after collection began; spending split 56% street repair and 44% safety projects; a Citizen Oversight Committee and annual audit.
- Voter approval in May 2016 (Measure 26-173) rests on OPB's report already cited on his brief: https://www.opb.org/news/series/election-2016/oregon-portland-gas-tax-passes/

## Not established here

- The resolution shows sponsorship and Council's referral. It does not by itself show the election result; that comes from OPB.
