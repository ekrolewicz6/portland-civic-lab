# Preschool for All: universal access board (added September 26, 2026)

Suggested by Dana at the Children's Institute (email, September 26, 2026): ask whether commissioners are committed to keeping universal access to preschool in Multnomah County, and what changes they expect in the coming years. Added as the `mult-pfa-universal` board for the chair and District 2 races, next to the tax-delay board. Dana was sent the draft wording and asked whether 2030 is the right benchmark and which public source best tracks seats, applications and waitlists; her answer was pending when the board went up.

## Context facts and their sources

- Definition and target: "universal coverage as having enough space for every 3- and 4-year-old whose family wants free preschool"; "it will need around 8,000 seats to attain universality in the coming years"; just under 14,000 county residents are 3 or 4 in 2026, falling to just over 13,000 by 2030 (medium scenario); coverage could come "much sooner" than 2030. Multnomah County news release, August 18, 2026: https://multco.us/news/news-release-preschool-all-ahead-schedule-universal-coverage-new-population-projections-show
- 2030 goal and 2026-27 demand: "universal preschool access for every 3- and 4-year-old in the County" by 2030; more than 6,000 applications for more than 7,000 seats. Multnomah County news release, May 26, 2026: https://multco.us/news/news-release-amid-record-enrollment-demand-multnomah-county-outlines-final-roadmap-universal
- Empty seats and waitlist: Auditor Jennifer McGuirk's letter to the Board (Wednesday, September 23, 2026), after a Good Government Hotline report whose fraud-and-waste allegation was not substantiated: 1,250 open seats as of August 13, about $2 million a month to pay for them; the program reported about 3,000 on its waitlist in August (the auditor's analysis puts the floor at 375 families); recommendations to make seat allocation data-driven and to tell families proactively what is open for 2027-28. Chair Jessica Vega Pederson's response cites a gap analysis finding open seats in outer East Portland and unmet demand in inner Southeast, Northwest and Southwest. Reported by Willamette Week, September 24, 2026: https://www.wweek.com/news/schools/2026/09/24/county-auditor-raises-concerns-around-preschool-for-all-contracting/ (the letter itself was not yet found on multco.us).
- About 7,100 seats allocated for 2026-27: same Willamette Week article; matches the tax-delay board's context.
